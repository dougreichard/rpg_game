# Gimme Dat Spoon: Strategic Edition

**Gimme Dat Spoon** is a fast-paced game of strategic attrition. Players compete to be the last one remaining by managing a limited pool of spoons. Through the use of unique Power Spoons, players can manipulate the flow of the game, sabotage opponents, or seize control of the turn order.

---

## Setup

* **Players:** 2 or more.


* **Equipment:** A standard six-sided die, and 12 spoons total.


* **The Spoons:** 6 spoons are **Power Spoons** (unique abilities) and 6 are **Standard Spoons** (no ability).


* **Starting Position:** Distribute the 12 spoons as evenly as possible among players.



---

## How to Play

1. **The Active Player:** The player holding the die is the Active Player. They roll the six-sided die to determine the target.


2. **Counting:** Count clockwise (or counter-clockwise if play has been reversed) around the circle, starting with the next player as "1." The count wraps around the circle.


3. **The Selection:** Before the count finishes, the Active Player must choose one spoon from their current hand to pass.


4. **Power Spoon Usage:**
* **Activate:** The Active Player may choose to trigger the spoon's ability immediately upon passing it.


* **Pass Unused:** The Active Player may pass the spoon without triggering it. The new recipient then holds the power and may choose to trigger it on a future turn when they are the Active Player.




5. **Depletion:** Once a Power Spoon’s ability is successfully triggered, it immediately loses its power and becomes a **Standard Spoon** for the rest of the game.


6. **Elimination:** If a player passes their final spoon, they are **out of the game** (unless protected by the Anchor Spoon).


7. **Turn Rotation:** Pass the die to the next player according to the current direction of play.



---

## Power Spoon Abilities

| Spoon Type | Ability | Strategic Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Anchor Spoon** | **Shield:** If you pass this and activate it, you are immune to elimination until the start of your next turn.

 | **Survival:** Use when down to your last spoon. |
| **Magnet Spoon** | **Swap:** If activated, pass this, then immediately take one spoon from the receiver.

 | **Growth:** The only way to increase your inventory. |
| **Spinner Spoon** | **Deflect:** If activated, the recipient spins the spoon; they must pass to the player it points to.

 | **Chaos:** Forces an opponent to execute a move for you. |
| **Switch Spoon** | **Hijack:** If activated upon receipt, you immediately become the Active Player.

 | **Control:** Seize the die and turn order. |
| **Reverse Spoon** | **Pivot:** If activated, changes the direction of play (clockwise $\leftrightarrow$ counter-clockwise).

 | **Tactical:** Keeps opponents off-balance. |
| **Anchorless Spoon** | **Exile:** If activated, discard this spoon from the game entirely instead of passing it.

 | **Reduction:** Safely remove a spoon without helping others. |

## Endgame: The Pile

Once only **two players** remain, the die works a little differently to keep
the finale from dragging out:

* **Rolling a 6:** Instead of passing, the Active Player chooses one spoon
  from their hand and discards it face-down onto **the pile** at the center
  of the circle. No Power Spoon ability triggers on a pile discard, even if
  the discarded spoon was a Power Spoon -- it just leaves the game.


* **Rolling 1-5:** Play continues as normal -- pass (and optionally activate)
  a spoon to the player the count lands on.


* **Elimination still applies:** if discarding to the pile empties a
  player's hand and they aren't shielded by an Anchor, they're eliminated
  immediately, the same as passing their last spoon.

### Determining the winner

The game ends once at most one player remains:

1. If the sole survivor is **shielded** (Anchor), that shield resolves first
   -- if it leaves them with an empty hand, they're eliminated too, leaving
   zero players standing.
2. **Zero players standing:** the pile wins.
3. **One player standing:** compare the pile's spoon count to that player's
   hand size.
   - Pile count **>= hand size** (including a tie): **the pile wins**.
   - Pile count **< hand size**: that player wins normally.

The pile can already hold spoons exiled earlier in the game by an
**Anchorless Spoon**, so a long game with several exiles can tip the
two-player endgame in the pile's favor even before the last roll of 6.

---

## Key Rules & Clarifications

* **Anchor Spoon Immunity:** If you pass your final spoon and activate the Anchor effect, you remain in the game with zero spoons until the start of your next turn. If you do not acquire a new spoon by the start of your next turn, you are eliminated.


* **Winning:** The game continues until at most one player remains holding spoons. See "Endgame: The Pile" above for how the two-player phase and pile-outs are resolved.


* **Inventory Scaling:** If playing with more than 12 spoons, any additional spoons must be Standard Spoons.
