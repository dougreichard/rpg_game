# Otis — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Sailor / dock worker, older. Broad-shouldered and weather-beaten, with the
steady economy of someone used to shipboard work. Roll-neck sweater, heavy
canvas trousers with a wide belt, sea boots. Salt-and-pepper beard kept trim,
deep crow's feet, calm steady eyes. Doesn't volunteer much — answers questions
directly and without fuss. Has a dry sense of humor buried under all that
weathering. Doug gave him a brass compass (engraved "To O.") years ago.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Blue-grey `#668CB2`

**Quest:** Wants the `brass_compass` back — lost in the harbor cargo shuffle;
gives the `sailors_knot_bracelet` as thanks.
