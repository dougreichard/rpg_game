# Penny — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Young adult, bright and chatty. Practical but colourful clothing — a
seamstress/mender: fitted top with a small pincushion bracelet on one wrist,
apron skirt with patch pockets, canvas shoes. Natural dark hair in two buns.
Quick smile, expressive eyebrows. Calls people "Hiya!" The most energetic
voice in the town roster. Dropped an embroidered handkerchief belonging to
Doug in the Old Parish Church and has been feeling guilty about it since.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Rose-pink `#D98CA6`

**Quest:** Wants the `embroidered_handkerchief` back — was mending it for Doug
when she dropped it; gives the `stitched_patch` as thanks on return.
