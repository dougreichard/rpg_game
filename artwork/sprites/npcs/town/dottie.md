# Dottie — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Cheerful, colourful, a little eccentric. Flowy skirt with a bold print,
layered tops, lots of small dangling accessories (charms, beaded bracelets,
a four-leaf-clover pin on her lapel). Round face, wide smile, hair in a
bright headscarf. An enthusiast who finds something lucky in everything.
"Ooh, hello!" energy. She collects lucky charms not because she's superstitious
but because she genuinely loves the idea of luck as a concept.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Warm pinkish-mauve `#B27A8C`

**Quest:** Wants the `rabbits_foot_keychain` to complete her charm shelf.
