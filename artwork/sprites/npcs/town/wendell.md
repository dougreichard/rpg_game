# Wendell — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Middle-aged man, introverted and understated. Soft knit sweater, plain slacks,
loafers. Slight build, a little rumpled. Quiet eyes that don't make much
contact; pauses before speaking. Not unfriendly — just not used to company.
Lives on the quiet fringe of town and collects ticket stubs as a solitary
hobby. His "...Oh. Visitors. Don't get many of those out here." opener
captures the whole character.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Earthy brownish-tan `#8C7266`

**Quest:** Wants the `ticket_stub_torn` back — from his collection, lost near
the Carnival.
