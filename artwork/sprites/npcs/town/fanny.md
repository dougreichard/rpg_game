# Fanny — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Middle-aged woman, quiet and introspective. Soft blouse, a light cardigan
she keeps pulling close, low-heeled shoes. Warm hair going slightly to grey,
kept loose to the shoulder. Gentle face with an air of preoccupation — she
mentions being "out of sorts." Doug gave her the lavender bottle the day
before he disappeared, and she's been carrying that weight since. Not sad
exactly — more like thoughtful, and waiting.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Warm olive-tan `#9E9973`

**Quest:** Wants `fannys_bottle` back — lost in the Underground Tunnels; Doug
gave it to her as a keepsake the day before he vanished.
