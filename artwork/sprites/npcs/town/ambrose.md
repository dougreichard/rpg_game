# Ambrose — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Middle-aged amateur scholar type — enthusiastic, slightly theatrical. Tweed
jacket with elbow patches, a waistcoat, wrinkled trousers, brogues. Broad
round face, wild eyebrows, a small pointed beard. Always looks like he's about
to explain something fascinating. Carries a rolled paper or small notebook. His
energy is entirely benign — he's just very excited about maps. Even bad ones.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Warm gold-tan `#99946B`

**Quest:** Wants the `faded_treasure_map` — purely to study it. "I'd love to
study it anyway. The X's never line up with anything, but still!"
