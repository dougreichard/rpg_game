# Clara — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Teenager or young adult, energetic and tinkery. Practical clothes with
gadgety pockets — cargo vest over a long-sleeve tee, rolled-up jeans, sturdy
lace-up shoes. One ear has small clip-on earphones looped around it (always
listening for something). Messy ponytail. The kind of person who says "Hey!"
immediately and gets straight to the point. She's building a crystal radio
from spare parts and takes both problems and solutions literally.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Cool blue-grey `#738C99`

**Quest:** Wants the `tangled_headphone_cable` back — last seen near the
Recording Studio; it's a spare part for her radio project.
