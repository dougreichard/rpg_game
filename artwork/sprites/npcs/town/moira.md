# Moira — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Older woman, warm and gentle. Cardigan over a floral blouse, sensible skirt
and flat shoes. Silver hair in a neat bun, soft round face, kind eyes behind
small glasses. The kind of person who calls everyone "dear." She knew Uncle
Doug and is quietly worried about him; her concern surfaces mid-quest when
she recognizes the "D." scratched on the skeleton key.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Muted teal-grey `#80948C`

**Quest:** Wants the `skeleton_key` back — she lent it to a friend (Doug)
long ago and never got it back.
