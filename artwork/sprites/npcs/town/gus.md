# Gus — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Older man, weathered and self-contained. Work jacket, plain trousers, heavy
boots. Sparse grey hair, deep-set eyes, slightly hunched posture from years of
hands-on work. Gruff but not unkind — the kind of person who says "eh?" before
every sentence. Knew Uncle Doug well; misses him but won't say so directly.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Warm tan-grey `#998C7A`

**Quest:** Wants the `bent_spoon` back — Doug used it to tap pipe seams.
