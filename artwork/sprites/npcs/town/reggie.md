# Reggie — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Younger adult — probably mid-twenties. Laid-back, a little scruffy in a
deliberate way. Graphic tee under an open flannel shirt, jeans, beat-up
trainers. Messy curly hair, easy grin. The "hey hey!" opener says everything.
Built an arcade cabinet from scratch with Uncle Doug; still tinkering with it.
The most casual, streetwise voice in the town NPC roster.

**Portrait color (in-game dialog):** Muted cool purple `#948099`

**Quest:** Wants the `arcade_token` back — custom-cast for the unfinished cabinet
he and Doug built together.
