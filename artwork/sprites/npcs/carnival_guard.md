# Carnival Guard — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Theatrical — this is a fairground so the guard wears an old-fashioned
circus-uniform: red jacket with gold braid, black trousers, flat cap with a
badge. Broad-shouldered but not genuinely threatening — more jobsworth bouncer
than actual danger. Guards the backstage curtain at The Carnival & Fairground.
