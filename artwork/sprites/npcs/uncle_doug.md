# Uncle Doug — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Middle-aged man — balding on top with a trim greying stubble beard, stocky
build, glasses on a chain. Rumpled collared shirt and slacks: he did not dress
for an adventure. Warm and slightly bewildered expression. Found in the
projection booth at the end of The Grand Marquee Cinema.
