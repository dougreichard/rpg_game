# Librarian — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

Strict, formal. Reading glasses on the end of the nose. Hair in a tight bun.
Cardigan over a collared shirt. Carries a large hardcover book or a stamp at
all times. The desk-blocker NPC in The Public Library & Archive. Her "step
aside" animation is the payoff for Erin's Fast Talk.
