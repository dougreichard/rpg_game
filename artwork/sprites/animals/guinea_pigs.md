# Guinea Pigs — Sprite Sheet

## Visual Design

A scurrying group of 3–4 guinea pigs per frame. Small round bodies, short
legs, varied colorings (brown, white, tan — classic guinea pig tri-color).
Their purpose is crowd-cover chaos — filling a floor with distracting
scurrying motion — so animations emphasize mass movement.

**Role:** Crowd cover. A scurrying group that floods the floor, drawing every
eye in the room and giving the duo a window to slip past or flank. Erin's
stealth sections are the natural pairing.
