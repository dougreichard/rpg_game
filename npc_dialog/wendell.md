# Wendell

- **Home tile:** (-5, 4) — open grass padding west of the map, beyond Bellows
  & Sons Pipe Organ Works
- **Personality:** A hermit-ish collector, quietly delighted by rare visitors
- **Quest id:** `wendell`
- **Wants:** Torn Ticket Stub (`ticket_stub_torn`) — already placed as a loot
  box at The Carnival & Fairground
- **Gives back:** nothing named — the reward is the story
- **Spoon reward:** Spoon No. 7 (`numbered_spoon_07`)

## Dialog

### Intro (not_started → active)
1. "...Oh. Visitors. Don't get many of those out here."
2. "I collect ticket stubs -- odd hobby, I know. Lost one somewhere near the
   Carnival."
3. "Torn clean in half. If you spot it, I'd love to add it back to the
   collection."
