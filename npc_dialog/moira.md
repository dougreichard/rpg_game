- **Home tile:** (16, 5) — open grass near The Public Library & Archive
- **Personality:** Warm town elder, nostalgic, a little wistful
- **Quest id:** `moira`
- **Wants:** Skeleton Key (`skeleton_key`) — already placed as a loot box in

- **Gives back:** nothing — the reward is the story
- **Connection to Uncle Doug:** the key is scratched with his "D." mark — the

  fit anything I've tried. — D.")
