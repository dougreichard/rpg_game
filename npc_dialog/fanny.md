# Fanny

- **Home tile:** (14, 16) — open grass near The Underground Tunnels
- **Personality:** Wistful, fond of lavender, a little out of sorts
- **Quest id:** `fanny`
- **Wants:** Fanny's Bottle (`fannys_bottle`) — already placed as the
  lantern + rubble-gated `_fanny_loot_box` in The Underground Tunnels
- **Gives back:** nothing — the reward is the story
- **Connection to Uncle Doug:** Doug gave Fanny the bottle "the day before he
  disappeared" — the most direct hint yet that something happened to him
