# Tobias

- **Home tile:** (15, -5) — open grass padding north of the map
- **Personality:** Mildly dazed, good-humored about being lost in the dark
- **Quest id:** `tobias`
- **Requires:** the Bellows & Sons Pipe Organ Works secret passage to have
  been found (`level_progress["pipe_organ_works"]["secret_revealed"]`) —
  Tobias doesn't spawn in town until then
- **Wants:** nothing — he has no fetch quest. The first conversation both
  "frees" him and completes his quest in one step
- **Gives back:** nothing named — the reward is the story
- **Spoon reward:** Spoon No. 11 (`numbered_spoon_11`), handed over
  immediately on the first conversation
