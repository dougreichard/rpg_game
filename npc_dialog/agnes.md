# Agnes

- **Home tile:** (15, 23) — open grass padding south of the map
- **Personality:** Unbothered, devoted organ practicer
- **Quest id:** `agnes`
- **Requires:** the Old Parish Church secret passage to have been found
  (`level_progress["old_parish_church"]["secret_revealed"]`) — Agnes doesn't
  spawn in town until then
- **Wants:** nothing — she has no fetch quest. The first conversation both
  "frees" her and completes her quest in one step
- **Gives back:** nothing named — the reward is the story
- **Spoon reward:** Spoon No. 12 (`numbered_spoon_12`), handed over
  immediately on the first conversation

## Dialog

### Intro (not_started → complete, in one conversation)
1. "Oh! Someone found the loft. I've been up there practicing for, oh,
   ages."

   out of my pocket."
3. "A spoon. Number '12', if you squint. A full dozen, all numbered, none

4. "Definitely a game piece -- but for which game, I couldn't tell you. Keep
   it!"

### After (complete)

   daylight."
